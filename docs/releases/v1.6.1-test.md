# v1.6.1-test

Test build of KairoTowns, for FM Towns games. It is not published to Google Play.

- Split Keyboard: on a screen 16:10 or wider, the on-screen keyboard splits into two halves that fill the empty space beside the game picture, which keeps its size. The Joystick page puts its directions on the left and its buttons on the right, and the touchpad has a surface in each half. Where the space beside the picture is too narrow for every key's label, the keyboard stays along the bottom. Turn it off in Controls › Keyboard › Split Keyboard.
- On-screen keyboard keys and tabs keep their labels on one line, shrinking them rather than breaking a word.

Install over v1.6.0 to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
