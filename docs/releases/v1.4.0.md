# v1.4.0

The first release of KairoTowns, a Fujitsu FM Towns emulator.

- Plays FM Towns CD and floppy games, with hard disk sets and user disks kept with their games, and Skip memory check (on by default).
- Games that start from a prompt get their startup commands typed for them.
- A catalog of FM Towns games with descriptions and artwork.
- Space and Return are on B and A by default, and every game's D-pad reaches the arrow keys and the joystick. Mappings follow a cascade: the FM Towns defaults, your all-games mapping, the game's catalog mapping, then your own for the game.
- Scaling anchor: Settings › Graphics pins the picture to any of nine places on the screen, separately for landscape and portrait, and each game's settings can choose its own. Pick it from a 3x3 grid.
- On a two-screen device (RG DS), the keyboard, joystick and touchpad are on the bottom screen, and the D-pad moves between the screens in the library.

Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
