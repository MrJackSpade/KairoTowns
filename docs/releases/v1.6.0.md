# v1.6.0

- A game's own Tsugaru settings now reach the machine.
- A hardware keyboard's symbols arrive as the characters you type, on the guest's own keyboard layout: `:`, `*`, `+` and the rest no longer come out as the key in the same place.
- A game's page shows its genres and number of players, from LaunchBox and MobyGames.
- On-screen keyboard: a symbol typed with Shift, such as `*` on the ?123 page, arrives as that symbol, and a quick tap is never lost.
- Game settings open at once in a large library: the Disks row no longer freezes the app.
- The Disks page shows its progress while a drive's disk is unpacked from an archive.
- On two-screen handhelds, a Bluetooth or USB mouse drives the game after a touch on either screen.
- On two-screen handhelds, a game's new box art or screenshot shows on the lower screen at once.
- A text setting's field stays above the Android keyboard.
- Third-party licenses open at once.
- Changing the app's language shows the new language at once.
- English counts read "1 game", "1 folder" and so on.

Install over v1.5.3 to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
