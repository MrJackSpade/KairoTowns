# v1.4.7

- Games that show a narrower picture than the FM Towns screen, such as Samurai Spirits, fill the screen instead of showing black at its sides and top.
- A new Gamepad setting in each game's Machine settings chooses the 2-button pad or Fujitsu's 6-button pad. Super Street Fighter II, Samurai Spirits and Fatal Fury Special use the 6-button pad by default; choose it in the game's own controller options too.
- With the 6-button pad, L1 and R1 press its C and Z, A, B, X and Y press its own A, B, X and Y, and the on-screen C and Z press its C and Z. The keyboard panel's Joystick tab shows the pad the game has.
- The Marty pad's ZOOM button is on the Joystick tab and can be mapped.
- The on-screen controls have C, Z, L3 and R3 buttons, hidden until you turn them on in Controls > Visible buttons. Button mapping lists them with the other buttons.
- A controller's stick presses are now the L3 and R3 buttons. By default they do what L1 and R1 do, and Button mapping can change them.

Install over v1.4.6 to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
