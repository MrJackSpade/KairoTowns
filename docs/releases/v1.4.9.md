# v1.4.9

- On-screen controls: a fast-forward button runs the game fast while you hold it. It is off until you turn it on under Controls > Visible buttons, and moves with the others under Arrange. A controller button can do the same under Button mapping.
- Typing on a Bluetooth or USB keyboard no longer stalls the app or loses keys.

Install over v1.4.8 to keep games, settings and saves. Both APK variants and the Play bundle have the same features.
